Deepvein Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Deepvein is a daily trivia game. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We keep it to the minimum needed for the game to work.

What we collect

You can play the daily dig without creating an account. When you use online features (scores, friends, leaderboards, online rooms), the app creates an anonymous account for you. We never ask for your email, phone number or real name.

For that anonymous account we store:

  • A random account ID and a friend code generated by the app.
  • A display name you choose. It's visible to your friends.
  • Your daily results: the dig number, your score, your result grid and the answers you gave. They're used for your friends' view and for showing your rank.
  • Your friends list: which players you have added with a friend code.

In online TNT Toss rooms, your display name and answers are sent live to other players in the room. Room activity is not saved after the game.

Your progress and settings are also stored on your device.

What we don't collect

  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone access.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • No analytics or third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Where it's stored

Online data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, which processes it only on our behalf.

Deleting your data

You can delete your online account at any time from the Profile screen in the app. This permanently removes your profile, daily results and friend links from our servers. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

Children

Deepvein does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app doesn't ask for any identifying details, and display names can be anything you like.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
