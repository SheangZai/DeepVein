Contact

Email timothy.devstudio@gmail.com with your question. If it's about a problem in the game, tell us your device, what you were doing and what went wrong.

Report or block a player

Tap a player's name in a TNT Toss room, on the ranked ladder or in your friends list, then choose Report or Block. Blocked players are hidden from you; you can unblock them from the Miner screen. We review reports and remove offensive names.

Delete your data

Open the Miner screen and choose Delete my online data. This permanently removes your online profile, scores, ranked record and friends. See the privacy policy for details.
